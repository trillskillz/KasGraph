# Code of Conduct

KasGraph is an open, welcoming project. We want participation to be a positive
experience for everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

## Our standards

We ask everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
other project space — to:

- Be respectful, considerate, and professional in all interactions.
- Assume good faith, and give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

Behavior that makes others feel unwelcome or unsafe is not acceptable. This
includes personal attacks, harassment, and any conduct that a reasonable person
would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when an individual is representing the
project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to
the maintainers through GitHub Issues or Discussions. Reports will be handled
confidentially. Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may take
appropriate action — including warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal from
project spaces — in response to behavior they deem inappropriate.
